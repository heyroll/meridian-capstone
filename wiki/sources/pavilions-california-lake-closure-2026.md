# Pavilions at California and Lake to Close After Decades Serving Pasadena Community

**Source:** [Updated: Pavilions at California and Lake to Close After Decades Serving Pasadena Community](https://www.coloradoboulevard.net/updated-pavilions-at-california-and-lake-to-close-after-decades-serving-pasadena-community/), Janine Tedros, ColoradoBoulevard.net

**Ingested:** 2026-09-16

## Key facts from the article

- Pavilions store #2224, at 845 E. California Blvd, Pasadena, is closing
  after nearly 50 years serving the community.
- Closure is expected to take effect **September 5, 2026**.
- Albertsons Companies (parent; store operates under the Southern California division, Vons)
  cited **lease expiration and financial underperformance** as the
  reasons — the company said it continuously evaluates store
  performance and that it is "occasionally... necessary to close
  locations that do not meet financial expectations."
- Pavilions is an upscale grocery format offering organic products, wine,
  and specialty items.
- The closure affects nearby residents and Caltech students. Neighboring
  businesses could see reduced customer traffic due to the closure.
- Albertsons said it would help affected staff find positions at nearby
  locations, per applicable labor agreements.
- The article raises an open question, not answered: whether
  **restrictive covenants** on the site could prevent a competing grocer
  from occupying the vacated space.

## Relevance to Meridian

This is a mixed signal for Meridian's Pasadena decision. On one hand, an
upscale/specialty-adjacent grocery format failing to sustain its lease
economics at this Pasadena location, after nearly 50 years, is a
cautionary data point about specialty grocery viability in this specific
market — worth weighing against the concurrent
[Sprouts expansion](sprouts-pasadena-expansion-2026.md), which points
the other way. On the other hand, the vacated space itself could be a
real-estate opportunity for Meridian, though the article's open question
about restrictive covenants means it's not yet known whether a
competing grocer (which Meridian would be, relative to Vons/Albertsons)
could even occupy it. Whether Meridian could pursue this site, and how
its closure should weigh against the Sprouts signal, are both open
questions the source does not resolve.
