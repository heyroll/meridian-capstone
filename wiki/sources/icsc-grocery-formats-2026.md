## Key facts from the article

- The U.S. grocery sector is valued at $912.4 billion, per an IBISWorld
  estimate — an estimate, not a verified count.
- Trader Joe's grew 3% year-over-year (through Feb. 28), 6 percentage
  points ahead of the overall grocery sector, with especially strong
  growth among Gen Z shoppers.
- Publix and Safeway are seeing pullbacks, with the sharpest declines
  among lower-income shoppers.
- Discount grocery growth has plateaued since mid-2025; the article does
  not project which direction it goes next.
- Smaller-footprint formats are an active industry trend: Gelson's is
  running "micromarkets" at 3,000–5,400 sq ft, paired with EV charging.
- Grocery-anchored retail center deals totaled roughly $11 billion in
  2025, up 42% from 2024.
- No definitive total count of grocery-anchored retail centers exists;
  the article cites JLL's tracking of 40,000 such centers as the closest
  available figure, not a verified total.
