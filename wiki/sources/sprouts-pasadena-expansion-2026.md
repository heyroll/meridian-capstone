## Key facts from the article

- A public notice at the former Rite Aid building (1421 E. Washington
  Blvd, at Washington Blvd and Hill Ave) indicates an application for an
  ownership change filed by SF Markets LLC, doing business as Sprouts
  Farmers Market. This would be Sprouts' first store within Pasadena city
  limits — approval is pending, not confirmed.
- Sprouts already operates a nearby location in unincorporated East
  Pasadena.
- A second location is planned for Highland Park, at a former 99 Cents
  Only Store (6235 York Blvd). The company has signed a lease, with an
  expected opening in 2027 (tentative).
- No square footage is reported for either location.
- Company-wide, Sprouts plans at least 40 new stores nationwide in 2026.
