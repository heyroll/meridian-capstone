# Census QuickFacts: Pasadena, California — Population & Income

## Population

- **Population, Census, April 1, 2010:** 137,122 — an actual decennial
  census count.
- **Population, Census, April 1, 2020:** 138,699 — an actual decennial
  census count.
- **Population estimates base, April 1, 2020, (V2025):** 139,618 — a
  revised starting figure the Census Bureau uses for its Vintage 2025
  intercensal estimates series. This differs from the actual 2020 census
  count above because of methodology revisions between vintages, not a
  data error — the two 2020 figures answer slightly different questions
  and neither replaces the other.
- **Population estimates, July 1, 2025, (V2025):** 135,804 — built from
  administrative birth, death, and migration data rather than a full
  count, and subject to revision in later vintages.

### Notable pattern in the data

Between the two decennial counts (2010 → 2020), Pasadena's population
grew slightly: +1,577 (+1.15%) over ten years. Between the 2020 figures
and the 2025 estimate, the trend reverses into a decline — -2,895
(-2.09%) measured from the raw 2020 census count, or -3,814 (-2.73%)
measured from the Bureau's own 2020 estimates base. Either way the
*direction* changes from slow growth to decline over the most recent
five-year window.

This section states that pattern only. No conclusion is drawn here about
its cause or its implications for Meridian's Pasadena decision — see
[`wiki/analyses/`](../analyses/README.md) if and when that analysis gets
filed separately.

## Income

- **Median household income (in 2024 dollars), 2020-2024:** $105,192 — a
  five-year American Community Survey (ACS) estimate, not a single-year
  figure.
- **Per capita income in past 12 months (in 2024 dollars), 2020-2024:**
  $65,304 — also a five-year ACS estimate, covering all individuals
  rather than households.
