## Key facts from the article

- Consumer preference for deli-prepared foods over restaurant meals more
  than doubled, from 12% in 2017 to 28% in 2025.
- 53% of Americans now take a hybrid approach to meals, combining
  deli-prepared options with items from their own kitchen.
- Retail foodservice dollar sales grew 1.6% to $52.1 billion over the
  past 12 months (as of publication).
- Shoppers purchase deli-prepared options most frequently between noon
  and 5 p.m.
- Allison Febrey (FMI senior manager, research and insights) frames the
  shift as shoppers "redefining value in food" — wanting quality and
  variety alongside time and money savings, not just convenience.
- Andrew Brown (FMI senior manager, industry relations) states that a
  "signature item" helps retailers ground their value proposition and
  reduce shopper decision fatigue.

**Note on the source itself:** this is a press release from FMI, the

but it is inherently boosterish toward the retail-foodservice trend it
describes — treat its framing (not its cited figures) with that in mind.
