# Deprecated conclusions

A running, dated record of conclusions that were declined during the
query/answer review process (see the Conclusion review rule in
[`wiki/SCHEMA.md`](SCHEMA.md)). Declined conclusions are moved here
rather than deleted, along with their original context — which page they
came from, their citation trail, the date added, and the date declined —
so they can be reconsidered later if new evidence changes the picture.
